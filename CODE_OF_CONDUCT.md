# Code of Conduct

We are committed to making participation in LeanToken, including this website,
welcoming and respectful. Be considerate, communicate constructively, and keep
discussion focused on the work.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and disruptive behavior are not
acceptable in project spaces.

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed to
protect the community. To report a concern privately, contact the maintainers
through GitHub.
